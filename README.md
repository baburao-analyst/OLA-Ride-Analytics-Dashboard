# 📌 Project Overview

The **OLA Ride Analytics Dashboard** is a comprehensive Business Intelligence solution developed in **Power BI** to analyse ride bookings, revenue trends, vehicle performance, ride cancellations, and customer experience.

The dashboard transforms raw ride booking data into meaningful business insights through interactive visualizations, KPI cards, DAX measures, and dynamic filtering, enabling stakeholders to make informed operational and strategic decisions.

---

# 🎯 Business Objectives

- Monitor overall ride performance
- Analyse booking and revenue trends
- Compare vehicle-wise performance
- Identify cancellation patterns
- Evaluate customer satisfaction
- Track business KPIs in real time

# 📊 Dashboard Pages

## 📊 1. Executive Performance Dashboard

<img src="Assets/Overall Dashboard.png" width="100%">

### Overview

The Executive Performance Dashboard provides a high-level overview of OLA's business performance by consolidating key operational and financial metrics into a single interactive view. It enables stakeholders to monitor booking activity, revenue generation, customer satisfaction, and ride performance while identifying overall business trends.

### Key Metrics

- Total Bookings
- Total Revenue
- Average Booking Value
- Average Ride Distance
- Average Customer Rating

### Business Insights

- Monitor overall business performance through executive KPIs.
- Track revenue trends to identify peak and low-performing periods.
- Analyze ride distribution across different vehicle categories.
- Identify top revenue-generating locations for strategic planning.
- Monitor customer satisfaction trends using average rating analysis.

---

## 🚗 2. Vehicle Type Analysis

<img src="Assets/Vehicle Performance Dashboard.png" width="100%">

### Overview

Analyze the operational performance of each vehicle category by comparing booking value, completed booking revenue, average ride distance, and total distance travelled. This page helps identify the most profitable and efficient vehicle segments.

### Key Metrics

- Total Booking Value
- Success Booking Value
- Average Distance Travelled
- Total Distance Travelled

### Business Insights

- Compare revenue contribution across all vehicle categories.
- Evaluate operational efficiency using average and total travel distance.
- Identify high-performing vehicle segments based on booking value.
- Support fleet allocation and service optimization decisions.

## 💰 3. Revenue Analysis

<img src="Assets/Revenue Dashboard.png" width="100%">

### Overview

Monitor revenue performance across multiple business dimensions, including payment methods, vehicle categories, booking status, and top customers. This dashboard provides a comprehensive view of revenue generation and financial performance.

### Key Visuals

- Revenue by Payment Method
- Revenue by Vehicle Type
- Top Customers by Revenue
- Daily Revenue Summary
- Revenue by Booking Status

### Business Insights

- Identify the most preferred payment methods contributing to revenue.
- Compare revenue generated across different vehicle categories.
- Track daily revenue trends and average booking value.
- Discover high-value customers based on booking revenue.
- Analyze the impact of booking status on overall revenue.

## ❌ 4. Cancellation Analysis

<img src="Assets/Cancellation Dashboard.png" width="100%">

### Overview

Explore ride cancellation patterns by examining customer cancellations, driver cancellations, incomplete rides, and vehicle-wise cancellation rates. This dashboard helps uncover operational issues affecting ride completion.

### Key Visuals

- Driver Cancellations by Reason
- Customer Cancellations by Reason
- Cancellations Over Time
- Cancellation Rate by Vehicle Type
- Incomplete Rides by Reason

### Business Insights

- Identify the primary reasons behind customer and driver cancellations.
- Monitor daily cancellation trends and overall cancellation rate.
- Compare cancellation rates across vehicle categories.
- Analyze incomplete ride patterns to improve operational efficiency.
- Support data-driven strategies to reduce ride cancellations.
